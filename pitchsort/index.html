HOW TO PLAY

Two target chords play at the start — one panned Left, one Right. Every note in the center column belongs to one of them. Sort by sound.

A hold = grab a note (it pulses) · move with ◀ ▶ · release = drop. B = cancel grab.
LT / RT replay target chords · X Y B audition columns.
START submit (center must be empty) · LB / RB volume.

Fewer moves = more stars. Perfect clears (3★) build your streak; sloppy clears (≤1.5★) cost one. Streak multiplies score, up to ×2 at streak 10.
